## Project summary
PollenID is a monorepo project that builds a practical Dutch guide to melissopalynology, designed primarily for beekeepers and secondarily for deeper study by palynologists. It reorganizes traditionally alphabetical or database-style information into a more intuitive, learning-focused structure centered on pollen types, keys, families, and monofloral reasoning. The content is written in Dutch and published with MkDocs, combining conceptual pages with extensive practical identification materials and references.
